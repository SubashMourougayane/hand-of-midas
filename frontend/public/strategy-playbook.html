The Core Edge: Liquidity Sweep Reversal

What We Trade

Price sweeps above/below a consolidation range (takes out stops), then REVERSES. We enter on the reversal candle (engulfing) and ride back into the range.

Daily Bias Filter

Why: Don't buy into a crash, don't short into a rally

If yesterday was a big red candle — only SHORT today. If yesterday was big green — only LONG. This single filter adds 10% to win rate.

Gold Micro

GOLDMICRO XAU/USD — Rolling 4hr Windows

Scans 24/7 (except 21-22 UTC market close). New consolidation window every 2 hours. Catches intraday reversals across all sessions.

Every 2 hours, a new 4-hour consolidation window opens (00-04, 02-06, 04-08...)
After consolidation ends, scan for 6 hours for a sweep ($2 above high or below low)
Once sweep detected, wait up to 45 minutes for an engulfing candle on M3 (3-min chart)
If engulfing confirms: ENTER at bar close + slippage
SL = sweep wick + $2 buffer | TP = opposite range edge - $2 buffer
Break-even: at 50% to TP, move SL to entry + $0.30
Max hold: 80 bars (4 hours) | Max 3 trades/day

Why 4 Systems?

Micro vs Macro: Micro catches more signals (rolling windows see patterns Macro's fixed Asia window misses). Macro catches the big Asia-to-London moves that Micro's tight 45-min window sometimes misses.

Gold vs Oil: Uncorrelated instruments. Gold crashes when Oil rallies (and vice versa). Running both = smoother equity curve, fewer drawdown days.
