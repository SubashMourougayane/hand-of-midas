## Verdict

Research-only. The fixed balanced candidate passes the static train/OOS, yearly, random, Monte Carlo, cost-stress, and neighborhood checks, but the automatic rolling walk-forward selector still produced negative test years. That means the edge is promising, but the selection process itself is not robust enough to promote as a live base yet.

The fixed balanced candidate is the better answer to "more PnL and more trades." The quality candidate is the better answer to "highest PF." Neither should be promoted until the walk-forward selection problem is solved.
