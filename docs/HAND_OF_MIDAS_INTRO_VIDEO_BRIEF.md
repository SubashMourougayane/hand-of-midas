## 1. The one-liner

**Hand of Midas is an autonomous gold-trading terminal — a proven quant strategy
that trades XAU/USD live, 24/5, and shows you every decision it makes.**

If the viewer remembers one thing: *it trades itself, and you can see everything.*

---

## 2. What the product actually is (so you brief accurately)

- A **live algorithmic trading system** for gold (XAU/USD) that runs on its own —
  no manual clicking, no chart-staring. The strategy was researched, back-tested
  over **20+ years** of data, and now runs on a real broker account.
- A **trading terminal** (web dashboard) that is the "face" — a dark, premium
  control room where the owner watches the machine work: live equity curve,
  positions, every trade, and *why* each trade was or wasn't taken.
- Its signature technical claim: **backtest = live**. The exact same code that was
  validated on 20 years of history is the code trading live — no re-implementation,
  no drift. "What we tested is what runs."
- **Rigor is the brand.** Every edge is hostile-audited for look-ahead / phantom
  results before it's trusted. The system is designed to *survive*, not to
  gamble — risk is actively managed so the account is never wiped.

### The screens you'll feature (real UI, already built)
- **Equity Curve** — a long green line climbing across 20 years, dollar P&L.
- **KPI cards** — Net P&L, Profit Factor, Win Rate, Max Drawdown, Positive Years,
  Trades. Big numbers, monospaced, gold-on-black.
- **Daily P&L calendar** — a month heatmap, green/red days (like a GitHub contrib
  graph but for profit).
- **Gate Funnel** — the "brain" view: millions of market events → pivots → setups →
  a handful of high-quality signals. Shows the system is *selective*, not spraying.
- **Live positions / trade blotter** — real tickets, entries, exits.
- **Multi-timezone clocks** — Sydney · Asia · London · New York · India. Signals a
  system that never sleeps and trades the global session.

> **On numbers:** for a trading-desk audience, numbers ARE credibility — use them.
> But only ones we can defend to a hostile trader. See **§2b** for the verified set:
> lead with the **structural** metrics (rock-solid), treat **absolute $** as
> "hypothetical backtest" with the standard guardrail. Never imply guaranteed or
> risk-free returns.

## 2b. Numbers for the video (verified — use these)

Source: the 20.3-year backtest run through the exact live code path (2006-03 →
2026-06, XAU/USD). All figures are **hypothetical backtest results** — always
pair on-screen numbers with the guardrail in §8.

### Hero metrics — lead with these (rock-solid, defensible)
| metric | value | how to use it |
|---|---|---|
| **Track record tested** | **20+ years** (2006–2026) | "Two decades of gold, tested." |
| **Positive years** | **21 of 21** | The headline stat. Every year in the black. |
| **Total trades** | **~28,000** | Scale / statistical significance. |
| **Win rate** | **~49%** | *Lean in:* "Wins under half its trades — and still profits." |
| **Backtest = Live** | **0-delta** | The unique, credible claim. Two curves snapping into one. |
| **Profit factor** | **~1.5** (backtest) | For every $1 lost, ~$1.5 won. |

### The growth story (frame as hypothetical)
- A **$5,000** account, risk-managed with monthly profit-taking, compounded into
  **six figures** across the 20-year backtest — *while never wiping the account*.
- Preferred on-screen phrasing: *"In a 20-year backtest, $5,000 grew into six
  figures — hypothetical, past performance is not indicative of future results."*
- ⏳ The **exact final $ + max-drawdown %** are being locked from the definitive
  (physical-accounting) run; drop them in when confirmed. Until then keep it to
  "six figures" — true and safe, no retraction risk.

### Supporting texture (optional on-screen)
- Exit character: most trades scratch at breakeven or take a small partial profit;
  the edge is a **few big winners** (fat right tail). Story: *"cuts losers, lets
  winners run."*
- Selectivity: **millions** of market events funnel down to **~1,400 trades/year**.

> **Rule of thumb for the editor:** a *count* (years, trades, win-rate) is safe to
> hard-cut on screen. A *dollar/percent-return* must carry the guardrail and, where
> possible, stay qualitative ("six figures") until the final figure is signed off.

## 3. Audience & tone

**Primary audience:** serious retail & semi-pro traders, and small prop/fund
operators — people who are skeptical of "trading bot" hype and have been burned by
it. They respect rigor, transparency, and drawdown control over flashy promises.

**Tone:** confident, calm, premium, *engineered*. Think **Bloomberg Terminal meets a
Swiss watch ad** — not "get rich quick," not neon crypto-hype. Understated power.
Dark, quiet, precise. The machine is doing serious work; the film is composed, not
frantic.

**Feeling to leave them with:** *"This is the real thing — built by someone who
actually knows what they're doing."*

## 5. Story / storyboard (60–90s hero cut)

Five beats. Adjust freely.

**Beat 1 — The problem (0–10s).** Cold open. A messy candle chart, a human hand
hesitating over a mouse, red P&L, 3am clock. Fast, anxious. VO/caption: *"Trading
is noise. Emotion. Missed sleep."* → hard cut to black + silence.

**Beat 2 — The reveal (10–25s).** The Hand of Midas logo/mark resolves in gold on
black. The dark terminal boots up — KPI cards populate, the equity curve draws
itself left-to-right, climbing. Calm returns. Caption: *"Meet the machine that
trades gold — so you don't have to."*

**Beat 3 — How it thinks (25–45s).** The Gate Funnel animates: a torrent of market
events pours in at the top, narrows through pivots → setups → and only a few
signals survive at the bottom. Caption: *"Millions of events. Only the highest-
quality trades survive."* Then a single clean trade fires on the chart — entry,
partial take-profit, exit — smooth, mechanical.

**Beat 4 — Why trust it (45–70s).** Split/overlay: the **backtest curve** and the
**live curve** snap together into one identical line. Caption: *"Tested on 20 years.
The exact same code trades live. Backtest equals live."* Then the multi-timezone
clocks tick across the bottom — Sydney to New York — conveying 24/5 autonomy and
global reach. Optional: a "drawdown guard" beat — the curve dips, the system
sizes down, survives, recovers. Caption: *"Built to survive, not to gamble."*

**Beat 5 — The close (70–90s).** Pull back to the full terminal, alive, glowing.
Logo. Tagline. CTA. Caption: *"Hand of Midas. Your gold, on autopilot."* (or the
approved tagline). End card with URL / waitlist.

## 6. Visual language

- **Palette:** near-black background (#0B0B0D-ish), **gold** as the hero accent
  (the Midas motif — turning the chart to gold), profit-green + loss-red only for
  data. Restrained. Let the gold and green do the talking.
- **Type:** a clean geometric sans for headlines; **monospace** for all numbers/
  data (it reads as "terminal / real data"). Numbers should feel *live* — count-up
  animations, ticking digits.
- **Motion:** precise, eased, mechanical — nothing bouncy. Lines *draw*, cards
  *snap*, funnels *flow*. Motion should feel like a machine executing, not a promo
  bouncing.
- **Signature motif:** *the Midas touch* — market noise/chart elements turning to
  gold as they pass through the system. Use sparingly, once or twice, as the hook.
- **Real UI first.** Whenever possible, feature the actual dashboard (screen
  captures), then enhance — don't invent fake screens that don't match the product.

## 8. Must-haves / must-avoids

**Must-have**
- The **backtest = live** idea, visually. It's the most credible, most unique claim.
- The **gate funnel / selectivity** — shows intelligence, not spam.
- The **premium terminal** look — this is a serious tool.
- Muted-safe captions on every cut.

**Must-avoid**
- ❌ *Unqualified* return claims — "% per month," "guaranteed," "risk-free," Lambos,
  cash fans. (Verified backtest numbers per §2b ARE fine — with the guardrail.)
- ❌ Neon crypto-bro energy, fast dubstep, stock-photo traders cheering.
- ❌ Fake/invented UI that contradicts the real product.
- ❌ Anything implying zero risk. The honest message is *managed* risk + survival.
