### Day 2: The Timezone Drift Bug (TDB) Discovery

**Trigger**: Why does Gold trade have R:R 0.46 when filter requires ≥0.8?

**Root cause traced** to `backend/execution/mt5_executor.py` line 216:

```python
"timestamp": t.replace(".", "-").replace(" ", "T") + "Z"
```

MT5 broker (JustMarkets) runs **GMT+3**. The above code takes server timestamps like `"2026.06.09 11:00:00"` (real UTC 08:00) and labels them as `"2026-06-09T11:00:00Z"` (claims to be UTC).

**Impact**: Strategy filters using `ts.hour` are off by 3 hours. Live's "Asia 0-8" is actually real UTC 21:00 prev → 04:59 today (NY close + Tokyo open, NOT real Asia). Live's "Scan 8-20" is real UTC 05:00 → 16:59 (Tokyo + London + early NY, cuts off late NY).

**20-year evidence** (simulated):
- Backtest signals: 1,077 (real UTC scan)
- Live signals (timezone-shifted): 962 (different signal set)
- 175 signals exist in live but NOT backtest
- 707 signals exist in backtest but NOT live
- Less than **18% overlap** between live and backtest behavior

**Today's two trades** are direct consequences:
- Oil LONG triggered with V1-only bias (since fixed to Combined)
- Gold SHORT triggered at 08:00 UTC because engulfing fired at server-09:42 (= real UTC 06:42, BEFORE backtest's scan_start of 08:00 UTC) — backtest never has this trade in its history

**Past week impact** (Jun 2-9):
- Gold Macro: 3 trades, +$411 (one was lucky win on broken R:R)
- Gold Micro: 6 trades, **-$1,140**
- Oil Macro: 1 closed (-$526) + 1 open
- Oil Micro: 0 trades
- **TOTAL: -$1,255 in 7 days** vs backtest expectation of positive PF 4-5 result
