THE THESIS
Liquidity sweeps the asleep, then reverses.

Asia hours form a tight range while Western markets sleep. When London opens, price often runs that range's extremes to harvest stops — and then turns. We trade the turn, not the run.

01
Range Forms

We measure Asia's session high and low. A rangebound block while the West sleeps.

02
Sweep Detected

Price punches through the range to clear stops, then closes back inside. A failed breakout.

03
Engulfing Confirms

Three-minute engulfing candle in the reversal direction. Now we have a setup.

04
Enter, Manage, Exit

Fixed stop. Half-position banked at half-target. Runner trails toward the structure exit.

EDGE ATTRIBUTION
Where the edge lives.
Directional bias

Yesterday's daily candle gates today's side. We don't fade conviction; we fade exhaustion.

Fill management

Half off at break-even·five. Trail behind structure on the runner. The biggest gains come not from picking — but from holding.

Drawdown discipline

Three losses cuts position in half. Five losses pauses the system entirely. The book recovers before it scales.

The numbers are public. The system is live.

STRATEGY PLAYBOOK
A liquidity sweep, then a fade.

One thesis underpins all four engines. Asia-session price action forms a tight range while Western markets sleep. When London opens, that range's extremes get tested — often violently. We trade the failed test, not the breakout.

THE SETUP
Three confirmations. One entry.

LONDON / NY OVERLAP
01
The range

Asia hours form a high and a low. We measure the box during the overnight window when liquidity is thin and conviction is low.

02
The sweep

Price punches through one extreme — running stops resting just above or below the range — then closes back inside. A failed breakout is the tell.

03
The engulfing

A three-minute candle in the reversal direction whose body engulfs the prior bar. Now we have alignment: range + sweep + reversal candle.

RISK FRAME
Small loss. Banked half. Trail the runner.

The hardest part of trend-following isn't finding the trade. It's holding it. Five filter modules in production make sure we lock in early profit and let the winning side breathe.

Fixed stop, anchored to structure

Stop loss sits a few points beyond the sweep wick — the level the market just rejected. If it gets retested, the thesis is invalid and we exit small.

Break-even at 35% to target

Once price moves 35% of the way to take-profit, the stop slides to entry. The trade becomes free.

Half off at 50% to target

At halfway, half the position closes for a guaranteed gain. The runner stays alive on a free trade.

Drawdown halve at three losses

Three consecutive stops cuts position size in half. Five pauses the system entirely. Books recover before they scale back up.

Per-bar deterministic slippage

Backtest fill model is bit-identical between runs. What you see in the historical equity curve is what live trading samples from.

WHY TWO ENGINES
One thesis. Two expressions.

The same pattern repeats across two markets. Rather than trade one of them well, we trade both with the same risk frame.

Gold Micro
ROLLING 4-HOUR WINDOWS

Same thesis on a faster timebox. Each four-hour consolidation produces its own range, swept and faded as a fresh setup. Multiple trades per day.

Oil Micro
ROLLING 4-HOUR WINDOWS

THE DISCIPLINE
What we won't do.
We don't curve-fit

Every filter that ships passes a 21-year out-of-sample backtest with a positive aggregate. Eight signal-pruning candidates that ‘felt right’ were rejected by data.

We don't override

No human discretion mid-trade. The strategy is fully deterministic. If a setup fires and the gates pass, the trade goes; if they don't, no manual intervention.

We don't chase sample size

Three losses in a row halves the size — without exception. The system is allowed to be wrong; we don't double down to break a streak.

We don't pretend

Backtests model spread, slippage, and broker maintenance halts. Live execution uses the same fill model. No phantom trade can pass our books.

Most systems lose by design.

Backtests overfit. Strategies based on intuition collapse. Position sizing scales ego. Slippage and fees compound silently. The five-year survival rate of retail algorithmic trading systems is rounding-error close to zero — and the survivors usually got lucky on regime, not skill.

Curve-fitting

Optimized to history. The first regime change wipes out the alpha. We checked: nine signal-pruning hypotheses tested, all failed when run on real 21yr data.

Hidden execution drag

Backtests assume zero slippage. Live broker spreads widen 3–5× during news. Theoretical PF 5 becomes live PF 2 once friction is honest.

Survivorship bias

The systems you read about are the ones that survived. The ones that died are silent. Honest validation requires testing on every year — including the bad ones.

Bugs are caught pessimistically.

When a class of bug is found, we don't just fix the one instance. We treat it as a pattern, audit all four backends in parallel, and write up every sibling we find. Recent example: a single dedup-guard typo led to a 24-issue silent-fail audit.

LIVE ↔ BACKTEST PARITY
The two paths must agree.

The live scanner and backtest engine are parallel implementations of the same strategy. A drift between them is a strategy bug — and they've happened. Every drift bug ever caught has a postmortem, a regression test, and a parity-harness check.

Parity harness

Runs in 25 seconds. Compares signal generation between live scanner and backtest signal generator across all 4 systems. Soft-gate fails build on catastrophic drift.

Calibration plan

Live execution drag (slippage, news spreads, downtime, broker rejection) is not in the backtest. Plan: 3-tier dashboard (Theoretical / Live Calibrated / Live Actual). Deferred until clean live data accumulates.

Parity-tested filters

Every shipped filter has run against the parity harness with 100% direction agreement and acceptable signal-count delta.

Replay harness — proposed

Future work: run live scanner code directly against historical OHLC, mock-broker fills, validate trade-by-trade against backtest. The definitive regression test for execution drift.

DEFENSE IN DEPTH
Multiple guards. Multiple layers.

One guard can fail silently for 18 days before it bites. The fix is layered guards that catch the same class of failure at different points in the pipeline.

Open-position dedup at scheduler level

Won't fire a new entry while one is open. Strategy-IN-list filter.

Open-position dedup at execute_signal level

Defense-in-depth. If scheduler check is bypassed (refactor, edge case), execute_signal still blocks.

Persistent sweep blacklist (DB-backed)

Once a sweep is consumed (taken or window-expired), DB row prevents re-fire — even after service restart.

Equity sanity floor ($100)

Refuses to size if account NAV is corrupt or below floor. Protects against bad broker data.

Position cap (5,000 barrels / 100 oz)

Hard cap on units regardless of equity. Truncates runaway compounding.

DD circuit breakers (3 / 5 losses)

Halve risk after 3 consecutive losses, pause entirely after 5. The book recovers before it scales.
