## PART C — BT vs LIVE PARITY: ⚠️ real divergences (the 1-week audit's job)
- Matched-window parity (2019-10+): A count 2.9%/net 4.2%, D count 5.2%/net 1.3% — within gate.
- **C1 (HIGH, CONFIRMED):** walker TIMEOUT exit never closes the broker position.
  `on_close` (live.py:573) does DB+sizer+reconcile, NO broker close. SL/TP are server-side
  (close at broker) but the per-leg hold-cap TIMEOUT (48/96 bars) has no broker equivalent →
  DB says closed, MT5 position runs on unmanaged until SL/TP hits. ~7% of A, ~2% of D exit by
  timeout. → orphan risk + phantom P&L until restart re-adoption catches it. **FIX: broker.close
  on EXIT_TIMEOUT.**
- **C2 (HIGH):** cost model differs — live books flat $0.65/trade; BT recomputes qty-scaled
  (~$31 at 2 lot). Sizer equity diverges; live UNDER-counts its own cost (BT is the more honest
  ledger). Compounds into future lot sizes.
- **C3 (HIGH):** sizer-reject handled oppositely — BT floors to 0.01 lot & trades; live drops the
  order. Different trade population at low/wiped equity.
- **H1:** walker close-based SL vs broker intrabar touch → wick-through disagreement (DB open,
  broker closed).
- **H3:** `max_open_positions` — BT counts this run's open_trades; live counts the WHOLE broker
  account (both legs + manual). Different gating with 2 separate processes.
- **H4:** `server_utc_offset` inference returns 0 on failure → shifts session gate + month-roll.

## PART D — SIZING MATH: math CLEAN, operational-state HIGH

- **F2 (HIGH, CONFIRMED):** EquitySizer NOT hydrated on restart. `cli.py:116` builds fresh at

## PART E — RECONCILE / P&L TRUTH: 1 HIGH + MEDIUMs

- **CLEAN (verified):** slow-ack recovery, partial-close slow-ack, breakeven-SL adoption, normal-path
